The Atopic Dermatitis appointment journey

A walkthrough of how the Ask Your Doctor avatar meets patients and caregivers at each stage, adapts its tone to where they are emotionally, and surfaces the right questions to bring to a real appointment. Frustration, and the push to escalate care, can surface at any stage, so it lives inside each one rather than as a separate step at the end. Toggle the lane, then step through the five stages.

Interactive prototype of the Ask Your Doctor atopic dermatitis journey for client demo. Toggle between caregiver and patient, then click any of the five stages to see the user's emotional state, where frustration can surface, how the avatar adapts its tone, the questions the session surfaces, and how sensitive topics are handled non-directively.

Viewing as

~80% of atopic dermatitis patients are age 5 or under, so the caregiver lane is primary. Every session opens with a short data-use consent (required because the tool captures research data for IRB), then routes by who you are and where you are. Non-directive throughout, plain language, grounded in input from Dr. Lio and a caregiver advocate.

Every session ends with a personalized PDF of questions to bring to the real appointment.
